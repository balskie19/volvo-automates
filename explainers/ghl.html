Follow-up that runs on its own.

The CRM is where the follow-up lives. These are the builds that decide who gets messaged, who gets left alone, and who is worth interrupting you for. Each one is a map rather than a film: tap any box to read what it does and watch what it writes on the contact light up. That last part is the point - these builds are only worth anything because of what they leave on the record.
