The jobs that have to wait, retry or loop.

n8n is where work goes when it cannot be done in one pass: a video that takes minutes to render, a call that must wait for opening hours, a list too long to fetch in one go. Each one plays from its trigger to its last step, the same shape as the canvas it was built on. Press play, or just scroll - they start when they reach you.
